Sample project

Design by talking to your CLI.

This page is a sample. Everything here can be changed from the chat on the right — or directly, without a model call.

1

Ask for a change

Type "make the headline warmer and add a pricing section" in the chat. The agent edits this file.

2

Point at things

Press C and click an element to comment on it, or E to select it — the selection rides along with your next message.

3

Edit in place

Press T and click any text to rewrite it. Ctrl+Z steps back through versions.

4

Share and hand off

Export to PDF, PowerPoint or Google Slides, share a snapshot link, or hand it to a coding session in your repo.

Delete this project whenever you like — it won't come back.
